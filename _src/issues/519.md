## Featured: The Dunning-Kruger effect in accessibility

"The people that can’t use it usually won’t file a bug report. They’ll just close your website or delete your app. So you don’t really get any signals telling you that something is wrong. And, a lack of signals, feels a lot like you’re doing everything right! I’ve been thinking about why this happens so often in accessibility, and it keeps bringing me back to an old study, and a term which gets thrown around a lot in product development. The Dunning-Kruger effect."

Read more of [The Dunning-Kruger effect in accessibility](https://www.craigabbott.co.uk/blog/2026/the-dunning-kruger-effect-in-accessibility/).
