## Featured: Atomic accessibility model

"That’s the trap. Not incompetence — arithmetic. A centralized model isn’t failing because the people in it are bad at their jobs. It’s failing because the workload scales linearly with the size of the estate while the team doesn’t scale at all. You can hire your way out of it temporarily, and then the estate grows again, and you’re back where you started with a bigger payroll. This is the problem the Atomic Accessibility Model exists to solve. Not by working harder, but by changing who does the work and where it happens. It starts with the idea of Hub-and-Spoke."
