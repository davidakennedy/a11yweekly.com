## Featured: It's time to retire the concept of decorative images

"WCAG says some images can be hidden from screen readers because they are decorative. We use that label too often. An image may not give us important information, but it can still affect how we understand or feel about a page. We should consider what an image adds before deciding that a screen reader should ignore it."

Read more of [It's time to retire the concept of decorative images](https://nicolas-steenhout.com/retire-decorative-images/).
