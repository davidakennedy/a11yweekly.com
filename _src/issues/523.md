## New to A11y

Okay, hear me out. Not exactly accessibility related, but a challenge you'll likely face in your accessibility work: How using LLMs will affect your work. A recent [study explores how AI use affects performance on math and reasoning tasks](https://news.berkeley.edu/2026/10/09/using-ai-for-just-10-minutes-erodes-your-ability-to-persist-at-hard-things/). The study "found that spending just 10 minutes relying on an AI tool significantly impairs our ability to focus and persist with difficult tasks." Much of accessibility work involves working through difficult tasks and decisions. Protect your ability to do that work.
