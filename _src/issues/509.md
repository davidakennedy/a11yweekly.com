## New to A11y

I'm a fan of what New York State has done with its design system, specifically how they've approached using AI with it. That's only part of the story though because a good design system allows you to [scale digital accessibility](https://equalentry.com/how-to-scale-accessibility/). Having a foundation in place, built on a design system has become more important than before, thanks to all the ways that system can get consumed, by humans or machines.
