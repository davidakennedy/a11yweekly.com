## Featured: I am not a developer or an accessibility specialist. Hire me?

"In any case, being asked to either code or design is not attractive to me, since it means doing either pointless or rote work. I simply cannot solve as many problems, as effectively, by being confined to either of these arbitrary and nonsensical camps. It’s debilitating. There’s also just so much more I’m able to do, if you let me be a whole person: typography and type design, animation, even sound design."

Read more of [I am not a developer or an accessibility specialist. hire me?](https://heydonworks.com/article/i-am-not-an-accessibility-specialist-hire-me/).
